Key features

Everything you need to accept payments and reconcile orders from inside Odoo.

Hosted redirect checkout

Redirect your customer to the Ecart Pay branded page. Card, transfer, cash and wallet methods are supported out of the box.

Signed webhooks

Every order status change is delivered as a signed HMAC-SHA256 webhook. The webhook secret is provisioned automatically when you save the provider.

Detailed line items

Products, shipping and discounts are forwarded as separate lines so your Ecart Pay orders match exactly the Odoo sale order or POS ticket.

Automatic setup

The webhook secret and the accounting payment method line are created for you when the provider is saved. Zero manual work on the Ecart Pay side.

Test & production

Switch between test and production with one click. Cached tokens are invalidated automatically whenever you rotate keys or change the mode.

Ready for the future

The Ecart Pay Odoo facade already exposes the surface for transparent checkout, payment intents, tokenization, recurring and wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Upgrade without changing your module.

4. Ecart Pay hosted checkout. The customer completes the payment on the Ecart Pay branded page with the method of their choice.

5. Confirmed payment. The customer is redirected back to Odoo and the transaction is marked as paid automatically.

6. Transaction detail. Every attempt keeps the reference, the Ecart Pay order id and the status for audit and reconciliation.

How it works

  1. The customer places an order on Odoo and picks Ecart Pay.
  2. Odoo creates the order through the Ecart Pay Odoo facade.
  3. The customer is redirected to Ecart Pay to complete the payment.
  4. Ecart Pay sends a signed webhook back to Odoo with the outcome.
  5. Odoo confirms or cancels the sales order automatically.
Requirements
  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise.
  • An Ecart Pay account with API keys (test and production).
  • Odoo instance reachable from the internet for webhooks.
